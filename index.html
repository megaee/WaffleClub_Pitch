Frequently asked

Can I skip or cancel anytime?

Yes. Skip your next box with 1 click, pause for 1–3 months at no cost, or cancel at the end of any month in My WaffleClub. You keep the iron.

What if I go on holiday?

Pause your plan for 1–3 months at no cost, or skip your next box. Pouches keep for 6 months unopened.

Is there a vegan option?

Yes. Flavours rotate every month, including vegan, gluten-free and savoury options. Berry Crush and Oat & Cinnamon are vegan. See vegan flavours.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Gift boxes for 1, 3 or 6 months, gift-wrapped with a card and delivered on your date. You pay once, no subscription for you. Gift boxes.
