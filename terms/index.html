Terms

Prototype terms for a university project. No contract is formed on this website.

1. Plans and prices

  • Single: €9.99 per month, 4 pouches. Family: €19.99 per month, 10 pouches. Party Box: €29, one box, any time.
  • The smart waffle iron costs €1 with every plan (Single and Family).
  • Gift boxes: 1 month €12.99, 3 months €29.97, 6 months €54.99, paid once. No subscription for the buyer.
  • All prices include 7% VAT. Delivery in Germany is free.

2. Subscription

  • After the first box, your plan renews at the plan price every 4 weeks (or every 2 weeks if you chose that rhythm).
  • Skip, pause (1–3 months, no cost) or change plan (Single ↔ Family) anytime in My WaffleClub.
  • Cancel at the end of any month. You keep the iron.

3. Right of withdrawal

You have a 14-day right of withdrawal on the iron and on unopened pouches.

4. Payment

PayPal, Klarna (pay later) and Apple Pay.

5. Referrals

Invite friends from My WaffleClub: when a friend starts a plan, you both get €5.
