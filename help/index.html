FAQ

Can I skip or cancel anytime?

Yes. Skip your next box with 1 click, pause for 1–3 months at no cost, or cancel at the end of any month in My WaffleClub. You keep the iron.

What if I go on holiday?

Pause your plan for 1–3 months at no cost, or skip your next box. Pouches keep for 6 months unopened.

Is there a vegan option?

Yes. Flavours rotate every month, including vegan, gluten-free and savoury options. Berry Crush and Oat & Cinnamon are vegan; Strawberry Sunday and Oat & Cinnamon are gluten-free; Cheddar & Herb is savoury. Filter flavours.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Gift boxes for 1, 3 or 6 months, gift-wrapped with a card and delivered on your date. You pay once, no subscription for you. Gift boxes.

Is Family enough for 4 people?

Yes! 10 pouches make about 40 waffles a month.

How does the iron know what to do?

Scan the pouch: the iron reads the QR code and sets heat and time. Add milk, shake, bake. Perfect waffles, zero measuring.

What does the iron cost, and do I keep it?

The smart iron costs €1 with every plan. If you cancel, you keep the iron.

Which payment methods do you accept?

PayPal, Klarna (pay later) and Apple Pay.

How long does delivery take?

Your first box ships in 2 days. Delivery in Germany takes 1–2 working days with DHL GoGreen and is free.
