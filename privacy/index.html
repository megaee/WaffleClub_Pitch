Privacy policy

This is a university prototype. It has no server-side database and sends no form data anywhere.

What this website stores

  • Your cart, the example account and orders you place are saved only in your own browser (local storage), so the shop works across pages.
  • Values you type into the checkout, gift and contact forms never leave your device.
  • You can delete everything by clearing this site's data in your browser, or with “Reset demo account” in My WaffleClub.

Third parties

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data.
  • There are no analytics or advertising cookies. In the real shop, KPIs would be tracked in GA4 only with your consent.

Payments

PayPal, Klarna and Apple Pay are shown as they would appear in the real shop. In this prototype no payment provider is contacted and nothing is charged.

Questions about privacy: contact us.
